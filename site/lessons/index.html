The course

Thirteen programs, from main to virtual threads. Each was compiled with every warning switched on and run; its page shows what it printed.

  1. 01Hello, WorldA class, a main method, one line printed.Java 1.0+
  2. 02Variables and typesEight primitive types, var, and what happens past the largest int.Java 10+
  3. 03Strings== compares objects, equals compares text, and strings never change.Java 15+
  4. 04Control flowif, a switch that returns a value, and a while loop.Java 14+
  5. 05MethodsStatic methods, overloading by type, and recursion.Java 1.0+
  6. 06ArraysA fixed-length row of values, sorted, and checked at every index.Java 5.0+
  7. 07Classes and objectsFields, a constructor, private state and toString.Java 5.0+
  8. 08Interfaces and polymorphismA contract, two classes that keep it, one loop over both.Java 9+
  9. 09Records, sealed types and patternsModern Java: data classes in one line, closed hierarchies, and switch on shape.Java 21+
  10. 10Collections and genericsList, Map and Set, and one method that works for any comparable type.Java 9+
  11. 11Exceptionstry, catch and finally; checked and unchecked; try-with-resources.Java 11+
  12. 12Lambdas and streamsFilter, map, sort and join a list in one expression.Java 16+
  13. 13Virtual threadsTen thousand tasks, each with its own thread, all waiting at once.Java 21+
