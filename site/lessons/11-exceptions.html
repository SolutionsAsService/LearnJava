Exceptions

try, catch and finally; checked and unchecked; try-with-resources.

Read the output in order: both calls to parse run, each printing its own lines, before println prints their sum, 42. finally runs whether or not an exception was thrown.

A checked exception such as IOException must be caught or declared with throws, or the program does not compile. An unchecked one such as NumberFormatException need not be. try (…) closes the reader automatically, even after an error.
